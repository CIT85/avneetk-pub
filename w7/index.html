Trailhead Ridge

Scenic mountain overlooks and coastal paths for local hikers

  • Winds along coastal bluffs and pine-lined ridges with sweeping views of the horizon.
  • Connects valley trailheads to high viewpoints with steady, rewarding elevation gain.
  • Best experienced during golden hour or crisp morning light for clear visibility.
  • Rewards steady pacing and solid footing with peaceful overlooks away from city noise.
